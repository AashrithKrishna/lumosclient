Sign in to use skins

Ely.by is a free skin system made for launchers like LumosClient. Create an account on Ely.by, then sign in here.

How it works
  1. Open the catalog and log in to Ely.by once (top right). LumosClient remembers it.
  2. Click ✓ on any skin to wear it right away. Nothing to download or upload. Have your own skin file? Use Upload my own skin.
  3. Close the catalog window. LumosClient refreshes your preview and uses it next time you play.
Cape
No cape on this accountEly.by gives capes to some accounts (it doesn't sell or hand them out). When your account has one, LumosClient shows it here and you wear it in game automatically.
Skins show for you and anyone else using Ely.by (LumosClient, TLauncher-style launchers, or servers with the Ely.by skin plugin). Horizon 26.3 doesn't draw custom skins yet.
