# Lumos Client 0.4.6

## New
- **Import from another launcher.** Lumos finds the instances of the official Minecraft Launcher, Prism Launcher, MultiMC, Modrinth App, CurseForge, SKLauncher and ATLauncher, and copies the ones you pick into new Lumos instances: worlds, mods (for a loader Lumos installs), resource packs, shader packs, settings, servers and screenshots. Your other launcher's files are never changed. A 26.3 instance becomes a Horizon 26.3 instance (Java mods are skipped, since they don't run there). Find it in the Library, the ⌘K palette and Settings > Storage.
- **A getting-started guide** on first start: your name or Ely.by account, instances, Discover, Horizon's native mods, folders, and opening the app on a Mac. Reopen it from ⌘K or Settings > About.

## In the latest all-in-one game (updates by itself)
- Lumos Map: a waypoint's name shows only with the crosshair on its marker, as in Xaero's.
