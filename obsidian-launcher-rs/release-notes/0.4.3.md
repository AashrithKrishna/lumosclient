# Lumos Client 0.4.3

## Fixed
- **Native mods disappeared from Discover:** on 18 November Horizon's mod registry moved all 137 native mods into an archive while it switches to a new mod format. Lumos now keeps a frozen copy of those listings (the downloads are unchanged and still checked by SHA-256), so Discover shows and installs them again.
- **Discover and native builds:** mods that can't share a game with the instance's are marked "Needs another instance", mods outside the all-in-one game say "Install (own game)", and the cards update right after an install or removal.

## New
- **Boost FPS for Horizon instances:** turns on Horizon's own entity, particle and hidden-leaf culling and its block-entity caches.
